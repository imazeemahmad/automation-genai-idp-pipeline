# .msg (Outlook) email parsing — olefile only.
# A .msg is an OLE/CFB container; we read attachment streams directly.
# NOT extract-msg: its RTFDE->oletools->pyopenssl chain pins cryptography<50,
#   conflicting with snowflake-connector-python's cryptography>=50 (arm64 layer
#   build fails with ResolutionImpossible).
# NOT msg-parser: its PtypBinary strips all 0x00 bytes from binary properties,
#   corrupting binary attachments (a PDF came back truncated and unreadable).
# olefile gives the exact raw bytes, is pure-Python and arm64-safe.
olefile>=0.47,<0.48
